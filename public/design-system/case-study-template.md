# Project title

## Project intro
One sentence describing what was designed or built and for whom.

## Problem / context
What needed to change? Keep this concise and specific.

## My role
State ownership, collaborators and constraints without overstating scope.

## Goal
Describe the intended user or business outcome.

## Research / insight
Show the evidence that influenced the direction.

## Design direction
Explain the core visual and interaction decisions.

## Exploration and iteration
Use wireframes, flows, rejected directions and before/after comparisons.

## Final design
Show the complete responsive experience with short captions.

## Technical implementation
Document reusable components, interaction patterns and delivery constraints.

## Outcome
Describe only outcomes supported by project evidence. Never invent metrics.

## What I learned
Capture one useful reflection and what would improve next.
